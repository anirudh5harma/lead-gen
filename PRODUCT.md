# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bombsell is for GTM operators, founders, and lean revenue teams who need to find lead opportunities, review outbound moments, monitor replies, and keep sending healthy without learning the technical substrate. The primary operator may be non-technical, so the product should feel like a calm daily workspace rather than a developer console or CRM admin surface.

## Product Purpose

Bombsell turns market signals into reviewed outreach and reply handling. Success means a user can understand what happened today, what needs attention, where replies are moving, and whether the system is healthy, while the AI-native architecture works quietly underneath.

The underlying model stays anchored to the planned architecture: Rep, Signal, Play, Conversation, and Outcome. The interface should present these as derived, human workflows only when they help the user make a decision.

## Brand Personality

Calm, autonomous, cinematic canvas. Bombsell should feel confident, composed, quietly alive, and precise. It is an intelligent workspace that works in the background and surfaces only the moments that matter.

## Anti-References

Avoid technical dashboards, CRM table sprawl, AI-purple gradient SaaS, developer-console language, raw infrastructure terminology, and dense system traces as the default experience. Avoid exposing internal models unless the user needs proof, review, or control.

## Design Principles

- Hide machinery until trust needs proof.
- Lead with work, not architecture.
- Keep the visible model small.
- Make autonomy legible and calm.
- Use the canvas as an abstract workspace, not an art effect.
- Preserve trust through clear review, inbox, sending health, and outcomes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. The experience must be reduced-motion safe and color-blind safe. Body text needs strong contrast, interactive controls need visible focus states, and status must never rely on color alone.
